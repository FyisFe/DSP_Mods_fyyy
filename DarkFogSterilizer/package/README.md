# DarkFogSterilizer

<details>
<summary>中文读我</summary>

一键清理指定恒星系的黑雾，并按当前黑雾设置下的星系巢穴上限，补满“绝育”太空巢穴。

- 清除目标星系的黑雾地面基地、建筑、部队、中继站和太空巢穴，包括尚未访问的行星上的基地。
- 清除目标星系派出的火种，以及其他星系已派往目标星系的在途火种。
- 每座新巢穴只保留一个有物质、零能量的未完工中枢核心。核心停止建造，不会吸引救援火种；满额巢穴占据星系容量，阻止新的火种殖民。
- 保留玩家建筑、矿物和已有地表坑洞、废墟。

### 使用

1. 打开星图，选中目标恒星、行星或巢穴，也可以进入目标的近景。
2. 点击星图底部中央的“绝育星系”按钮。
3. 在确认弹窗中核对星系名称和最终核心数量，点击“确认绝育”执行；点击“取消”或按 Escape 退出。

适用于启用黑雾的单人存档。保存后，绝育状态会随存档保留，移除模组后仍有效。再次摧毁核心或通过其他模组改变核心能量、巢穴容量，可能使黑雾恢复扩张。

操作有风险，请提前备份存档。

<summary>README</summary>

Clear the Dark Fog from a selected star system and fill its hive capacity, as determined by the current Dark Fog settings, with dormant space hives.

- Removes the target system's Dark Fog ground bases, structures, units, relays and space hives, including bases on unvisited planets.
- Removes tinders launched by the target system and incoming tinders from other systems.
- Each new hive contains only an unfinished core with matter but zero energy. It cannot advance construction or attract rescue tinders. Filling the system's hive capacity prevents new tinder colonization.
- Preserves player structures, mineral deposits, existing surface pits and ruins.

### Usage

1. Open the starmap and select the target star, planet or hive, or enter its close-up view.
2. Click the “绝育星系” button at the bottom center of the starmap.
3. Check the system name and final core count in the confirmation dialog. Click “确认绝育” to proceed, or “取消” / Escape to cancel.

For single-player saves with Dark Fog enabled. Once saved, the dormant hives persist even after removing the mod. Destroying their cores or using other mods to change core energy or hive capacity may allow the Dark Fog to expand again.

This operation carries risk. Back up your save beforehand.
